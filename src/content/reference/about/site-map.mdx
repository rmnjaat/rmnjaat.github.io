The claim of this site is that every page of the original outside the blog is covered here. This page is the proof. The left column lists every URL of the English original, taken from its sitemap and table of contents; the right column names the page here that covers its content, or says why there is none. Pages map one to one. The reference appendices are reorganised: the original splits each engine's execution plan appendix into three pages, this site has one page per engine with the same three parts as sections, and the original's example scripts are per engine and chapter where this site has one schema page.
